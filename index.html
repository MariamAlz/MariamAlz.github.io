Master's thesis · Khalifa University · 2022 to 2024

Neuromorphic GAN-assisted computing architecture for image dehazing in underwater navigation

Underwater, light scatters and fades fast, so cameras see a blue-green haze. That makes navigation hard for underwater robots. I trained GAN-based dehazing models on underwater datasets and built them with spiking neural networks, the brain-inspired models that run efficiently on neuromorphic hardware. The goal is clearer images in real time, using little power.
